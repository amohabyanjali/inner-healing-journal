# Content Structure - Amoha Inner Child Healing Journal

## Overview

This document details the complete content structure of the Amoha By Anjali Inner Child Healing Journal, a 30-day guided journey to self-compassion and healing.

## Journal Specifications

- **Title**: Inner Child Healing Journal
- **Subtitle**: A 30-Day Journey to Self-Compassion
- **Brand**: Amoha By Anjali
- **Tagline**: "Not just Fortune telling but Soul awakening"

- **Structure**: 4 Weeks + Completion
- **Contact**: amohabyanjali@gmail.com | amohabyanjali.com

## Weekly Themes

### Week 1: Meeting Your Inner Child
**Days 1-7**
- Focus: Initial connection, setting healing goals
- Key Activities: Visualization, EFT for safety, doodling, body awareness, letter writing

### Week 2: Recognizing Patterns
**Days 8-14**
- Focus: Identifying childhood patterns and breaking them
- Key Activities: Pattern work, EFT for shame, butterfly coloring, butterfly hug, inner child letters

### Week 3: Expression & Release
**Days 15-21**
- Focus: Authentic expression and emotional release
- Key Activities: EFT for worthiness, affirmation coloring, heart breathing, letters to younger self

### Week 4: Reparenting & Integration
**Days 22-28**
- Focus: Integrating healing and becoming your own loving parent
- Key Activities: EFT for integration, hand mapping, vagal toning, future self letters

### Week 5: Completion
**Days 29-30**
- Focus: Reflection and commitment to ongoing healing
- Key Activities: Journey reflection, final commitment letter

### Weekly Rhythm Pattern

**SUNDAY**: Week review & integration (Days 7, 14, 21, 28)

**THURSDAY**: Somatic practice (Days 4, 11, 18, 25)

**SATURDAY**: Gratitude & celebration (Days 6, 13, 20, 27, 30)
